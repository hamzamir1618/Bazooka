صاروخبازوكا
THE SAROUKH, IN 14 FLICKS

BAZOOKA is a shawarma brand built around the Saroukh (صاروخ, “rocket”): the long, tightly rolled, foil-wrapped saj wrap that street vendors in Cairo, Beirut and Damascus call a rocket or a bazooka.

The landing page treats scrolling as an adventure. Each flick of the wheel sets off a physical contraption (a cast-iron press, a carving sword, a rotisserie, a matchbox, a night shutter), all styled as a hand-printed 1968 Cairo cinema poster.

HOW TO REVIEW

  1. Press Open full prototype (top right) and view it full-screen. The reel on the right is the same page, shrunk.
  2. Each flick of the mouse wheel, swipe or ↓ key plays one beat. The whole story is about 14 flicks.
  3. Things to try: hover the hero to tilt the wrap, click a matchbox, and press Tear ticket on the receipt at the end.
  4. Prefer free scrolling? Switch off SNAP in the top-right corner of the prototype.

    Your call

    Feedback wanted

    The direction is set; these are the open decisions before we build the production site.

    Pacing

    Is 14 flicks the right length?

    The menu orbit takes one flick per dish (5 flicks) so each card can be read. We can show two dishes per flick, or let people skim the menu sideways.

    Scroll control

    Keep “one flick = one beat”?

    Snapping makes every flick land on a finished moment, but takes scroll control away from the visitor. The SNAP toggle shows the free-scroll alternative.

    Placeholder copy

    Prices, tagline & claims

    Prices (Rs 850–1,450 per wrap), the tagline “أقوى صاروخ في البلد”, the “A direct hit” review line, “since 1968” and the dish descriptions are placeholders to confirm.
